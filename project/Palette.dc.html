Early prototype
This is Palette.
One place to log everything you read, watch and listen to — books, film, TV, records, podcasts and nights out — and to find what’s next through people rather than an algorithm.
It’s a working prototype, so some things are half-built and some will break.
Have a proper poke around — log something, open a title, wander into Discover.
Tell me what works and what doesn’t. The bad feedback is the useful kind.
